How It All Began

Asha Jyothi began in June 2007 when Saji and Cynthia John recognised the need for quality, accessible skill training that could open real doors to employment and financial independence.

What started small has grown into an organisation dedicated to delivering quality vocational education that empowers women and youth to find jobs, build livelihoods, and achieve lasting career success.

Since then, Asha Jyothi has trained more than 16,800 learners, equipping them with certified, practical, employment-ready skills and supporting them on their journey to stable work and independent futures.

Questions Students Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about courses, fees, eligibility and admissions. Still unsure? Call any campus and we will guide you.

What courses does Asha Jyothi offer?

Asha Jyothi offers 16 job-oriented courses. Healthcare: Medical Nursing Assistant, Medical Lab Phlebotomy and Pharmacy Assistant. Computers: Computer Basic (MS Office), Desktop Publishing (DTP) and Tally Prime and Data Entry. Technical trades: Electrical and Electronics and Home Appliance Repair. Beauty and tailoring: Beautician (Basic and Advanced), Tailoring (Basic and Advanced) and Maggam and Electric Machine. Language and teaching: Spoken English (Level 1 and 2) and Pre-Primary Teachers Training.

Are the courses free? What are the fees?

All courses are subsidized. Asha Jyothi is a non-profit training centre, so fees are kept low so that every deserving student can afford to learn. The fee depends on the course. Call your nearest campus for the current fee.

Which courses can I join after 10th or Inter?

With a 10th or 12th (Inter) pass you can join Medical Nursing Assistant, Medical Lab Phlebotomy, Pharmacy Assistant, Pre-Primary Teachers Training, Electrical and Electronics, and Tally Prime and Data Entry. Tailoring, Beautician (Basic), Spoken English (Level 1), Computer Basic and Home Appliance Repair are open to everyone, with no minimum qualification.

How long are the courses?

Most courses take 3 to 4 months. The healthcare courses (Nursing Assistant, Phlebotomy and Pharmacy Assistant) take 6 months.

Will the course help me get a job?

Every course is built around a real job. Students train for work in hospitals, diagnostic labs, pharmacies, offices, salons, boutiques, schools and appliance service centres, or to start working for themselves. Most class time is spent practising on real equipment, so students leave ready to work. Ask the admissions team what job support is available for your course.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Students who complete a course receive a course certificate. Asha Jyothi is recognized by the Intermediate Board of Telangana. Certification details vary by course, so the admissions team can tell you exactly what your course offers.

Where are the campuses?

Asha Jyothi has three campuses: Alwal (Dwarakapuri Colony, Kompally Road, Secunderabad 500010, phone 80196 80291), Medchal (Athvelly, near Shantha Biologics, Medchal 501401, phone 77803 46642) and Ramanthapur (opposite Ithika Shaantham Meadows, Wadiaram 502255, phone 99498 09263).

How do I join?

Call the campus nearest you, visit in person, or fill in the enquiry form on this page. An admissions counsellor will help you pick the right course and tell you when the next batch starts.
