Saji K John & Cynthia John

Founders

"What began with two students became a lifelong commitment to quality skill education, dignified livelihoods, and brighter careers for all."

How It All Began

Asha Jyothi began in June 2007 when Saji and Cynthia John recognised the need for quality, accessible skill training that could open real doors to employment and financial independence.

What started small has grown into an organisation dedicated to delivering quality vocational education that empowers women and youth to find jobs, build livelihoods, and achieve lasting career success.

Over the years, Asha Jyothi has trained hundreds of aspiring learners — equipping them with certified, practical, employment-ready skills and supporting them on their journey to stable work and independent futures.

Our Distinctive Strengths

Why Choose Asha Jyothi?

A decade and a half of dedicated service delivering tangible skills, affordable education, and disciplined training.

18+ Years of Proven Impact

Operating since 2007, we have empowered thousands of aspiring learners across Telangana with practical, employment-ready skills, dedicated mentorship, and real career opportunities.

Subsidized Training

Our non-commercial, community-subsidized pricing ensures that quality skill training stays within reach of every deserving family.

Practical Lab-First Skill Training

Classes focus extensively on actual machines, sewing units, salon equipment, electrical test benches, and clinical simulation rather than theoretical lectures alone.

16 Certified Courses

Tailoring, Cosmetology, Spoken English, Teacher Training, Technical Trades, Healthcare, and Computers under one roof.

Institutional Affiliation Note

The institute holds official recognition by the Intermediate Board of Telangana for its foundational operating credentials. Specific vocational short-term certifications vary by subject syllabus and lab curriculum.
